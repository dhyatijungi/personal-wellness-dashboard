Circadian Science

The 90-Minute Sleep Cycle Matrix

Human sleep is governed by ultradian cycles lasting approximately 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle eliminates morning sleep inertia.

Stage 1 & 2

Light Sleep (NREM)

Heart rate slows and core body temperature drops. Muscle relaxation begins, transitioning the nervous system away from active daytime beta waves.

50% of Total Sleep Time
Stage 3

Slow-Wave Deep Sleep

The most restorative phase. Human growth hormone releases, tissues repair, cellular waste clears via the glymphatic system, and immune memory consolidates.

20-25% of Total Sleep Time
Stage 4

REM (Rapid Eye Movement)

Vivid dreams and cognitive consolidation. The brain synthesizes emotional experiences, problem-solving memory traces, and neuroplastic pathways.

20-25% of Total Sleep Time

The 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Reset

When mental stress or anxiety surges during exam or work pressure, reconnect to physical reality using your five senses:

✋ 4 Things You Feel:

👂 3 Things You Hear:
