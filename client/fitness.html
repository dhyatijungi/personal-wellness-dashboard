💡 Golden Rules for Safe & Effective Training

1. Progressive Overload

Gradually increase reps, time under tension, or decrease rest periods to stimulate ongoing adaptations.

2. Adequate Hydration

Drink 500ml of water 30 minutes prior to workouts to prevent cramping and maintain intracellular fluid balance.

3. Respect Rest Days

Muscles grow and repair during sleep and active recovery, not during the workout itself.
